M.S. Thesis / Structural Mechanics

Stochastic Topology Optimization & System Reliability

The Challenge

Evaluating structural vulnerability under random vibration events requires assessing the probability of system outcrossing rates. Standard topology optimization often assumes deterministic loading, missing critical probabilistic failure modes.

Analysis & Methodology

  • Developed custom MATLAB algorithms mapping finite element models under stochastic loading.
  • Utilized Rice's formula and Lyapunov covariance updates to evaluate outcrossing rates across multivariate domains.
  • Implemented 2D elastostatic elements and Newton-Raphson solvers to maintain structural accuracy during optimization loops.
Tools: MATLAB | Newton-Raphson | Reliability Theory
